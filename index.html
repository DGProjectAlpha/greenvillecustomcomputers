Our Process

From first chat to a machine that is ready to use

No black box. You know what we are ordering, why it fits your budget, and when it will be in your hands.

  1. 1 / 5 Start

    Free consultation

    Tell us what you need the PC for, what you already own, and what you are comfortable spending. We listen first, then sketch a plan together.

  2. 2 / 5 Plan

    Decide on parts

    We land on a parts list that matches the job — not a stack of extras. If something is overkill for your use, we will say so.

  3. 3 / 5 Order

    Order the parts

    Once you are happy with the list, we order. You stay in the loop on timing and any swaps if stock shifts.

  4. 4 / 5 Build

    Assemble on site or at our shop

    We put it together where it makes sense for you — at the shop, or at your place if that is easier. Cable management included.

  5. 5 / 5 Finish

    Set up and delivery

    OS, drivers, a quick walkthrough, and handoff. You leave with a working machine and a clear picture of what is inside it.
